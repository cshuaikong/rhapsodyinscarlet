## Where did the developers talk about the game?

KONAMI shared the deepest look at *Rhapsody in Scarlet* so far across three channels around the September 3, 2026 announcement: the State of Play reveal, a sit-down interview with **Co-Producer Takanori Murayama** during State of Play Japan, and the **KONAMI PRESS START** broadcast. Murayama also authored a PlayStation.Blog post explaining the game's design philosophy.

<Video id="qaYFGhohwEE" title="KONAMI PRESS START 9.3.2026 — Rhapsody in Scarlet segment" />

## What is the combat design philosophy?

Murayama noted that many recent action games center on **sword-based melee combat** — and that the team deliberately set out to challenge themselves with the **freedom that only magick allows**. Combat combines close-range strikes, long-range attacks, and wide-area magick, with **beast abilities woven directly into combos** to make attacks stronger and flashier. The goal is for each player to invent their own fighting style. For a breakdown of how this works, see our [combat system guide](/gameplay/combat-system/).

## How was 1920s New York recreated?

The team aimed to reproduce the atmosphere of 1920s New York in detail — from architecture and fashion to cars and **Prohibition-era speakeasies**. Murayama described the era as full of new energy: cars and radio were spreading rapidly, and women were entering society in new ways. The team conducted **on-site location scouting in New York** and collected period materials, then fused that realism with fantasy elements like beasts, magick, and the magick district to create the game's distinct world.

## Who voices Ellie and Ilya?

The broadcast confirmed both the English and Japanese voice casts:

- **[Ellie Myers](/characters/ellie-myers/)** — Cherami Leigh (English) / **Akari Kito** (Japanese)
- **[Ilya Jones](/characters/ilya-jones/)** — Hannah Grace (English) / **Saori Onishi** (Japanese)
