## What is the setting of Rhapsody in Scarlet?

*Rhapsody in Scarlet* is set in **New York City during the 1920s** — a dazzling metropolis filled with the sound of jazz and the glow of soaring skyscrapers. While the city revels in its glamour, an unseen conflict simmers among those who practice magick in secret.

## What is the main plot?

The story follows **Ellie Myers**, a young magus who arrives in New York to pursue a career as a jazz singer. Her arrival leads to an unexpected incident that draws her into the city's hidden magical underworld. This encounter brings her to **Ilya Jones**, leader of the **Seekers** — an elite organization of magi.

As Ellie and Ilya investigate the schemes of a **mysterious mafia**, their investigation draws them ever deeper into the city's secrets. What begins as a simple case gradually reveals a **fateful battle that threatens the very balance of the world**.

## Who are the main factions?

### The Seekers
An elite organization of magi led by Ilya Jones. They operate from the **Sorcellerie Quarter** and work to maintain order among secret magic practitioners in New York.

### The Mysterious Mafia
A shadowy criminal organization whose schemes drive the central conflict of the story. Their true goals and connections to the magical world are gradually uncovered throughout the narrative.

### The Sorcellerie Quarter
Headed by **Harold** (Ilya's father), this district serves as the base of operations for the Seekers and represents the formal magical establishment in New York.

## What themes does the story explore?

The narrative explores several interconnected themes:

- **Freedom vs. Order** — Represented by Ellie and Ilya's clashing philosophies
- **Friendship and Partnership** — Two unlikely allies learning to trust each other
- **Destiny** — A world-shaking conspiracy that threatens everything
- **The Hidden World** — Magic existing beneath the surface of everyday 1920s New York

## How does Ellie and Ilya's relationship develop?

Initially, the free-spirited Ellie and the serious-minded Ilya frequently clash due to their opposing worldviews. However, as they fight side-by-side through many battles, they gradually forge a strong bond and come to understand one another. Their evolving partnership is central to the story's emotional core.

## Is Rhapsody in Scarlet connected to other KONAMI games?

There is no confirmed connection to other KONAMI franchises at this time. The game appears to be a standalone original IP developed by **KONAMI Osaka Studio**.
