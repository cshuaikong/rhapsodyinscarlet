## How does combat work in Rhapsody in Scarlet?

Combat in *Rhapsody in Scarlet* is built around the **bond between magus and beast**. Players fight alongside powerful beast companions and string together spectacular combo attacks fueled by magick. The core loop emphasizes mixing different ability types to overwhelm enemies.

## What types of abilities are available?

The game features an **expansive arsenal of [abilities](/abilities/getting-started/)** that can be mixed and matched:

| Ability Type | Description | Combat Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Close-range attacks** | Melee strikes and short-distance magick | High damage, risk/reward |
| **Long-range attacks** | Projectiles and focused magick beams | Safe positioning, sustained damage |
| **Area-of-effect (AoE) skills** | Wide magick bursts and explosions | Crowd control, multiple targets |

## What is the beast system?

Magi fight alongside **beasts** in combat. Confirmed by KONAMI:

- **Combo integration** — Beast skills weave directly into magick combo chains
- **[Beast switching](/abilities/beast-switching/)** — Switch between beasts mid-combat to access different abilities
- **Spectacular visual attacks** — The combination produces flashy, high-impact combat sequences

[Ellie](/characters/ellie-myers/)'s signature beast companion is **[Sköll](/beasts/skoll/)**, with [Balhut](/beasts/balhut/) and [Thunderbird](/beasts/thunderbird/) also named in the announcement trailer.

## How do combos work?

KONAMI describes the design as giving players freedom to invent their own fighting style. The confirmed system lets you combine magick and beast abilities across close-range, long-range, and area-of-effect attacks, then switch beasts as the encounter changes. The game has not yet published a required combo order, damage values, cooldowns, or a best build.

The sequence shown in the image is an official visual reference, not a tested input guide. Until the game is released, treat specific timings, ability effects, and matchup advice as unconfirmed.

## What genre is Rhapsody in Scarlet?

The game is an **Action Adventure** title with a heavy emphasis on real-time combat. It is developed by **KONAMI Osaka Studio** and features a narrative-driven experience set in 1920s New York.

## What platforms will the game be on?

*Rhapsody in Scarlet* is confirmed for:
- **PlayStation 5**
- **Xbox Series X|S**
- **Steam (PC)**

## Is there multiplayer or co-op?

No multiplayer or co-op modes have been announced at this time. The game appears to be a single-player experience focused on the story of Ellie and Ilya.

## Gameplay Tips for Beginners

- **Experiment with ability combinations** — the official design goal is player-created fighting styles
- **Learn the role of each beast** — switching is confirmed, but each beast's detailed abilities are not yet public
- **Use the three ranges as a planning model** — close, long, and area attacks are confirmed categories, not published damage rankings
