## What types of abilities are in Rhapsody in Scarlet?

*Rhapsody in Scarlet* features an **expansive arsenal of magick abilities** that players can mix and match during combat. KONAMI's official announcement confirms three attack ranges:

| Ability Category | Combat Style | Best Used Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Close-range attacks** | Melee strikes, short-distance magick | Single tough enemies |
| **Long-range attacks** | Projectiles, focused magick | Mobile or distant enemies |
| **Area-of-effect (AoE) skills** | Wide magick bursts | Groups of enemies |

*The attack ranges are officially confirmed; the "best used against" matchups are general action-game guidance, not published stats.*

## How do abilities work with beasts?

Confirmed by KONAMI: players **combine magick with beast abilities**, and beast skills can be **woven directly into combo chains**, making attacks stronger and flashier. Players can also **[switch between multiple beasts](/abilities/beast-switching/)** mid-combat — [Sköll](/beasts/skoll/), [Balhut](/beasts/balhut/), and [Thunderbird](/beasts/thunderbird/) are confirmed so far — to access different abilities as situations change.

## Can abilities be customized?

KONAMI has not detailed a customization or upgrade system yet. Co-Producer Takanori Murayama frames the design goal as letting players **create their own fighting style**, and the confirmed beast-switching mechanic implies loadout-style choices between beasts. Specifics — skill trees, upgrades, unlocks — remain unknown.

## What is magick in the game's world?

Magick in *Rhapsody in Scarlet* is practiced in secret by a hidden community in 1920s New York. The game's setting blends the glamour of the jazz age with an unseen world of magical conflict, where organizations like the **Seekers** work to maintain order among magic practitioners.
