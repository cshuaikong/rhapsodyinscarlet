## What types of abilities are in Rhapsody in Scarlet?

*Rhapsody in Scarlet* features an **expansive arsenal of magick abilities** that players can mix and match during combat. The three primary ability categories are:

| Ability Category | Combat Style | Best Used Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Close-range attacks** | Melee strikes, short-distance magick | Single tough enemies |
| **Long-range attacks** | Projectiles, focused magick beams | Mobile or distant enemies |
| **Area-of-effect (AoE) skills** | Wide magick bursts, explosions | Groups of enemies |

## How do abilities work with beast companions?

The magick system is deeply tied to the **magus-beast bond**. Abilities are not just cast by the player character — they are **channeled through the beast companion**, creating a unique combat dynamic where:

- The beast **amplifies** the magus's magick power
- Coordinated attacks produce **spectacular combo sequences**
- Different ability types can be **chained together** for devastating effects

## Can abilities be customized?

While specific customization details have not been fully revealed, the emphasis on "mix and match" suggests players will have flexibility in building their ability loadout. The game encourages experimentation with different combinations of close-range, long-range, and AoE skills.

## What is magick in the game's world?

Magick in *Rhapsody in Scarlet* is practiced in secret by a hidden community in 1920s New York. The game's setting blends the glamour of the jazz age with an unseen world of magical conflict, where organizations like the **Seekers** work to maintain order among magic practitioners.

## Abilities Quick Reference

| Type | Description | Strategy Tip |
|---|---|---|
| **Close-range** | Melee and short magick strikes | High risk, high reward; use when safe |
| **Long-range** | Projectiles and magick beams | Maintain distance, sustained DPS |
| **AoE** | Wide-area magick bursts | Clear crowds, control battlefield |

## Tips for Ability Mastery

1. **Mix all three types** — The combo system rewards variety, not spamming one attack
2. **Watch enemy patterns** — Match ability types to enemy behavior
3. **Coordinate with your beast** — Time abilities with beast companion attacks for maximum impact
4. **Experiment with loadouts** — Try different combinations to find your preferred playstyle
