## What is beast switching? (Confirmed)

Beast switching is a core combat mechanic in *Rhapsody in Scarlet*. According to KONAMI's official announcement, players can **switch between beasts mid-combat to access different abilities** and respond to changing combat situations. This was further demonstrated during the KONAMI PRESS START broadcast, which showcased the flexible combo system combining magick and beasts across close-range, long-range, and area-of-effect attacks.

## Which beasts are confirmed? (Confirmed)

Three beasts have been named in official materials so far:

- **[Sköll](/beasts/skoll/)** — [Ellie Myers](/characters/ellie-myers/)' signature beast companion
- **[Balhut](/beasts/balhut/)** — named in the announcement trailer
- **[Thunderbird](/beasts/thunderbird/)** — named in the announcement trailer

Whether more beasts will be revealed — and whether [Ilya Jones](/characters/ilya-jones/) commands her own beasts — has not been officially stated.

## How do beast skills fit into combos? (Confirmed)

Co-Producer Takanori Murayama explains that **beast skills can be woven directly into combo chains**, making attacks stronger and flashier. Combined with the [three attack ranges](/abilities/getting-started/) (close-range, long-range, AoE), players are encouraged to adapt their fighting style to each enemy and situation rather than repeating a single pattern.

## What is not yet known

KONAMI has not yet revealed:

- Which abilities belong to which beast
- Whether switching is instant, on a cooldown, or resource-gated
- How beasts are unlocked or upgraded
- Whether beasts have elemental affinities or weaknesses

## What we expect (Speculation — not official)

Based on how the system is described, a reasonable guess is that **each beast maps to a different combat range or role** — for example, one beast excelling at close-range combos while another covers AoE crowd control. Sköll, as the signature companion, will likely be the most balanced. Treat this as inference, not fact, until KONAMI confirms per-beast abilities.
