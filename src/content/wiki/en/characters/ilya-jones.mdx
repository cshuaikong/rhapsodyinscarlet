## Who is Ilya Jones?

Ilya Jones is the young leader of the **Seekers**, an elite organization of magi in *Rhapsody in Scarlet*. Voiced by **Hannah Grace**, she is an exceptionally talented magus who possesses both remarkable skill and a strong sense of responsibility.

## What is Ilya's background?

Ilya was raised under the tutelage of **Harold**, her father and the head of the **Sorcellerie Quarter**. This upbringing instilled in her a deep commitment to order and discipline. As leader of the Seekers, she works to maintain balance among the secret practitioners of magick in 1920s New York.

## How does Ilya fight?

As a highly trained magus, Ilya demonstrates exceptional combat skill and tactical prowess. While specific details about her beast companion and abilities are yet to be revealed, her background suggests she employs a disciplined and strategic approach to magick combat.

## What is Ilya's relationship with Ellie Myers?

Ilya and Ellie represent opposing philosophies: Ilya values **order** while Ellie values **freedom**. This fundamental difference leads to frequent clashes between the two. However, as they fight side-by-side through many battles, they gradually come to understand one another and forge a powerful partnership.

## Who is Ilya's voice actor?

Ilya Jones is voiced by **Hannah Grace**.

## What is the Seekers organization?

The Seekers is an elite organization of magi led by Ilya. They operate in the shadows of 1920s New York, managing the unseen conflict among those who practice magick in secret. The organization's base is in the **Sorcellerie Quarter**, headed by Ilya's father, Harold.
