## Who is Ellie Myers?

Ellie Myers is the protagonist of *Rhapsody in Scarlet*, a young magus who arrives in 1920s New York to pursue a career as a jazz singer. Voiced by **Cherami Leigh**, Ellie is a free-spirited individual who values freedom above all else and refuses to be bound by rules or authority.

## What is Ellie's background?

Ellie comes to New York with dreams of becoming a jazz singer, but her path quickly diverges when she becomes caught in an unexpected incident involving the city's hidden world of magick. This encounter leads her to **Ilya Jones**, leader of the **Seekers** — an elite organization of magi dedicated to maintaining order among magic practitioners.

## How does Ellie fight?

Ellie channels the powers of both **beasts** and **magick** to unleash coordinated attacks in combat. Her beast companion, **Sköll**, fights alongside her, enabling spectacular combo attacks fueled by magick. The bond between magus and beast is central to the game's combat system.

## What is Ellie's personality?

Ellie is defined by her free-spirited nature and unconventional thinking. She frequently clashes with the serious-minded Ilya, who values order above all else. Despite their differences, the two gradually forge a strong bond as they investigate the schemes of a mysterious mafia together.

## Who is Ellie's voice actor?

Ellie Myers is voiced by **Cherami Leigh**, a prolific voice actress known for roles in anime and video games.

## What beast companion does Ellie have?

Ellie fights alongside **Sköll**, her beast companion. The bond between magus and beast allows them to string together spectacular combo attacks and unleash powerful magick abilities.
