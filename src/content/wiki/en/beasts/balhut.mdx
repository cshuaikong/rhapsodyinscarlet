## What is Balhut?

**Balhut** is one of the beasts featured in *Rhapsody in Scarlet*. In the announcement trailer, protagonist [Ellie Myers](/characters/ellie-myers/) calls on three beasts by name: **Sköll**, **Balhut**, and **Thunderbird** — confirming that Ellie can command multiple beasts in combat, not just her signature companion.

## What is Balhut's role in combat?

KONAMI has confirmed that combat is built around **switching between beasts** to access different abilities and respond to changing combat situations. Balhut's specific abilities, attack style, and element have not yet been officially detailed.

## Who is Balhut bonded to?

This has not been officially confirmed. [Sköll](/beasts/skoll/) is Ellie Myers' signature beast companion; whether Balhut is bonded to Ellie, [Ilya Jones](/characters/ilya-jones/), or another magus remains unknown.
