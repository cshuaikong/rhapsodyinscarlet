## What is Thunderbird?

**Thunderbird** is one of the beasts featured in *Rhapsody in Scarlet*. In the announcement trailer, protagonist [Ellie Myers](/characters/ellie-myers/) calls on three beasts by name: **Sköll**, **Balhut**, and **Thunderbird** — confirming that players can switch between multiple beasts during combat.

## What is Thunderbird's role in combat?

KONAMI describes the combat system as chaining **close-range, long-range, and area-of-effect attacks** by switching between beasts. Thunderbird's specific abilities and where it fits in that system have not yet been officially detailed.

## Who is Thunderbird bonded to?

This has not been officially confirmed. [Sköll](/beasts/skoll/) is Ellie Myers' signature beast companion; whether Thunderbird is bonded to Ellie, [Ilya Jones](/characters/ilya-jones/), or another magus remains unknown.
