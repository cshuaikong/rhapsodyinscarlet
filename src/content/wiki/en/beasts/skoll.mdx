## What is Sköll?

**Sköll** is the beast companion of **Ellie Myers**, the protagonist of *Rhapsody in Scarlet*. The bond between Ellie and Sköll is central to the game's combat system, enabling spectacular combo attacks fueled by magick.

## How does Sköll fight?

Sköll fights alongside Ellie in combat, allowing them to string together **coordinated combo attacks**. The beast companion amplifies Ellie's magick abilities and enables visually spectacular combat sequences.

## What is the magus-beast bond?

The bond between a magus and their beast companion is a core mechanic of *Rhapsody in Scarlet*:

- **Combat coordination** — Beasts and magi work together in real-time combat
- **Magick amplification** — The beast channels and enhances the magus's magical power
- **Combo enablement** — The bond allows for extended attack chains that neither could achieve alone

## Are there other beast companions?

While Sköll is confirmed as Ellie's beast companion, details about other beasts in the game — including Ilya's potential companion — have not yet been revealed by KONAMI.
