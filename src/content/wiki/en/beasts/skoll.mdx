## What is Sköll?

**Sköll** is the beast companion of **[Ellie Myers](/characters/ellie-myers/)**, the protagonist of *Rhapsody in Scarlet*. The bond between Ellie and Sköll is central to the game's [combat system](/gameplay/combat-system/), enabling spectacular combo attacks fueled by magick.

## How does Sköll fight?

Sköll fights alongside Ellie in combat, with **beast skills woven directly into her combo chains** — and Ellie can [switch between beasts](/abilities/beast-switching/) mid-combat to adapt. Sköll's specific abilities have not yet been officially detailed.

## What is the magus-beast bond?

The bond between a magus and their beast companion is a core mechanic of *Rhapsody in Scarlet*:

- **Combat coordination** — Beasts and magi work together in real-time combat
- **Magick amplification** — The beast channels and enhances the magus's magical power
- **Combo enablement** — The bond allows for extended attack chains that neither could achieve alone

## Are there other beast companions?

Yes. The announcement trailer revealed that Ellie calls on three beasts by name: Sköll, **[Balhut](/beasts/balhut/)**, and **[Thunderbird](/beasts/thunderbird/)**. Sköll is her signature companion; the other beasts' abilities and bonds have not yet been officially detailed.
