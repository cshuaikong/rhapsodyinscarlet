## When was the story trailer released?

The **Story Trailer** for *Rhapsody in Scarlet* was released on **September 3, 2026**, the same day as the announcement trailer and the official website launch.

## What does the story trailer reveal?

The story trailer provides deeper insight into the narrative:

- **Ellie's journey** from aspiring jazz singer to magus caught in a world-shaking conspiracy
- The **conflict between freedom and order** represented by Ellie and Ilya
- The **mysterious mafia** and their schemes in 1920s New York
- The **Seekers organization** and the hidden magical underworld
- The **Sorcellerie Quarter** and the formal magical establishment

## How does it differ from the announcement trailer?

While the announcement trailer focused on introducing the game, setting, and combat system, the story trailer delves deeper into:

- Character relationships and emotional beats
- The central mystery and conspiracy
- The world-building of the magical underworld
- The stakes of the conflict

## Where can I watch the story trailer?

The trailer is available on the [official KONAMI website](https://www.konami.com/games/ris/) and KONAMI's official YouTube channel.
