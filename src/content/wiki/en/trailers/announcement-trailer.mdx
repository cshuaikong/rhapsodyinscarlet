## When was the announcement trailer released?

The **Announcement Trailer** for *Rhapsody in Scarlet* was released on **September 3, 2026**, alongside the launch of the game's official website.

## What was revealed in the announcement trailer?

The announcement trailer introduced:

- The game's **1920s New York setting** with jazz-era aesthetics
- The two protagonists: **Ellie Myers** and **Ilya Jones**
- The **magus-beast combat system** featuring spectacular combo attacks
- The **Seekers organization** and the hidden world of magick
- The game's **action-adventure genre** and real-time combat

## Where can I watch the announcement trailer?

The trailer is available on the [official KONAMI website](https://www.konami.com/games/ris/) and KONAMI's official YouTube channel.

## What platforms were confirmed?

The announcement trailer confirmed *Rhapsody in Scarlet* for:
- **PlayStation 5**
- **Xbox Series X|S**
- **Steam (PC)**
