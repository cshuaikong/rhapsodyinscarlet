## What is the Rhapsody in Scarlet theme song?

**"Cool/Hot"** is the theme song of *Rhapsody in Scarlet* — a swing-infused jazz track performed by Japanese jazz band **SOIL & "PIMP" SESSIONS** together with singer-songwriter **Emi Meyer**. The song's swinging sound matches the game's 1920s Jazz Age New York setting, where protagonist [Ellie Myers](/characters/ellie-myers/) pursues her dream of becoming a jazz singer.

## Who are the artists behind Cool/Hot?

- **SOIL & "PIMP" SESSIONS** — a Japanese jazz band known for their explosive "DEATH JAZZ" style. The lineup features Shacho (agitator), Tabu Zombie (trumpet), Josei (piano), and Akita Goldman (bass), with support members Takeshi Kurihara (saxophone) and Seiya Onasaka (drums) on this track. The band received the BBC Worldwide Award "John Peel Play More Jazz Award" in 2005 and has performed at festivals worldwide, including Fuji Rock, Glastonbury, and Montreux.
- **Emi Meyer** — a singer-songwriter born in Kyoto and raised in Seattle, active in both Japan and the US. Her debut album *Curious Creature* earned her Best New Artist recognition in the jazz category on major streaming platforms.

## Who wrote Cool/Hot?

The lyrics were written by **Emi Meyer**, the music was composed by **Shacho**, and the arrangement is by **SOIL & "PIMP" SESSIONS**.

## How does the music connect to the game?

KONAMI states that jazz-inspired music accompanies players throughout the adventure, capturing the energy of the Jazz Age. Music is also woven into the story itself — [Ellie Myers](/characters/ellie-myers/) is an aspiring jazz singer, and her partnership with [Ilya Jones](/characters/ilya-jones/) unfolds against New York's nightlife.
